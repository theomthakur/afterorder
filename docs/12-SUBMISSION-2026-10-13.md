## Track

**Trust, Identity & AI Infrastructure.** It's about bounded agent authority: what an agent is allowed to spend, who verifies delivery, and an evidence trail for every purchase.

## Name and tagline

**AfterOrder**: from order to fulfilment, with agents accountable for every purchase.

## Short description (one line)

AfterOrder turns a paid Shopify order into a bounded supplier purchase. A team of agents sources offers, applies the merchant's rules, funds an order-specific escrow on Monad and recovers on its own when a supplier fails.

## Description

Small merchants who fulfil orders through suppliers repeat the same manual loop: check stock, compare prices, pay, chase dispatch and update the store. When the cheapest supplier fails, they do it all again. Handing this to an AI agent is risky unless the agent's spending power is capped and every payment is tied to verified delivery.

AfterOrder gives purchasing agents that authority with limits enforced onchain:

- **Bounded mandates on Monad.** The merchant sets a per-order cap, a total budget and an allowlist of suppliers. The contract enforces them. An agent cannot pay an unapproved supplier or exceed the limit, and the merchant can revoke authority at any time.
- **Order-specific escrow.** Funds lock per order and are released only against signed dispatch evidence from a verifier the merchant chose. The purchaser cannot pick its own verifier.
- **A manager agent with specialists** for sourcing, policy, reservation, settlement, fulfilment and reconciliation. The manager's choices are made by an LLM through **OpenRouter** (Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite). The model can only pick from offers that the policy code has already approved. Any invalid or unparseable model answer is rejected, and the deterministic rule takes over.
- **Autonomous recovery.** In the demo, Northstar quotes the lowest price but its last unit is gone. The manager switches to Harbor Works without raising the merchant's 40 DemoUSD limit.
- **A restart-safe workflow.** Every step is checkpointed. A failure pauses at its checkpoint and resumes without creating a duplicate order.
- **A 3D command center** that shows the full journey live, including failures and replanning, plus a downloadable proof report for each order.
